# Privacy policy

*IPTV Enhanced, for Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Apple TV, and Android phones and tablets. Last updated 26 October 2026.*

IPTV Enhanced does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The developers receive nothing from the app: it has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your device

The app stores what you enter and what you do in it on your device only:

- the playlists you add: their addresses and, for Xtream logins, your username and password;
- the channel list and TV guide downloaded from those playlists;
- favourites, hidden and locked items, watch history and playback positions;
- your settings, and your parental PIN, which is stored only as a salted hash.

Deleting a playlist removes its data. Uninstalling the app removes everything, except the Apple TV Keychain copy described below.

## Where the app connects

The app only connects to the addresses in your playlists: your provider's servers, the guides and channel logos they list, and the streams you play. These requests go directly from your device to those servers, which see your IP address and, for Xtream logins, your username and password, as with any player. Their own privacy policies apply. The developers don't run any server that the app talks to.

## Backups

- **Android:** your playlists and preferences are included in Android's own device backup only when that backup is end-to-end encrypted (your device has a screen lock). Android handles the backup; the developers can't read it.
- **Apple TV:** a copy of your playlists and preferences is kept in the Apple TV's Keychain on the device, so it can be restored if the system clears the app's storage. It is not synced to iCloud. The Keychain can keep it after the app is uninstalled; deleting your playlists in the app before uninstalling removes them from it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone. The parental PIN lets you lock channels and groups.
