A TV guide for the playlists you already have.

Free and open source, for Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV and Apple TV. Add your M3U playlist or Xtream login and browse your channels in a real TV guide, made for the remote.

Now

Made for the remote

Everything works with the arrow keys and OK, and Back always closes the innermost thing first.

  • The guide comes first

    It opens on a full programme grid with a live preview, so you see what's on before you switch.

  • Catch-up

    Go back to earlier programmes on channels that offer it, straight from the guide.

  • Movies and series

    Pick up where you stopped, with the next episode ready when one ends.

  • One search

    Find channels, programmes on air, movies and series from the same box.

  • Big playlists, no lag

    Tens of thousands of channels and guides over 100 MB stay smooth.

  • Nothing leaves your TV

    No accounts, ads or tracking. Your playlists and settings stay on the device.

On your TV

    Bring your own playlist

    IPTV Enhanced comes with no channels. It plays the playlists and logins your provider gives you, and you should only add ones you're authorised to use.

    The channels in these screenshots are made up for the demo.
